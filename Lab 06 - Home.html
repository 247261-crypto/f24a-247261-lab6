How to submit

This is separate from the teaching. Read it before you start the tasks.

You will make one GitHub repository named f24a-YOURROLL-lab6. One page lives in that folder. The file name is on your project. You still do the work in Git during the lab.

Copy server.js from the session folder into that same folder. Do not change server.js. In that folder, run:

node server.js

The terminal prints http://localhost:3016/. Open your page from that address. A page opened from the folder icon cannot ask the store computer.

Your project number is on Tasks. The three projects sit on the session bar after Tasks. Open the one with your number.

The last hour is the work at your PC. The instructor marks that work in the room.

Save report.docx. Your name, your roll number, this lab’s title, your project number, what you finished in your words, and one screenshot of your page showing the check on your project. You do not commit report.docx.

The work is marked in the room on Friday. Upload YourName-YourRoll.docx on the Classroom Assignment Lab 06 — A page that waits by Wednesday 21 October 2026, 16:00. This class uses Classroom, not a Drive folder.

After each topic is done, commit, then continue.

git add .

Use the commit message on the topic you just finished. The report is due Wednesday 21 October 2026, 16:00.

The three projects

The class has three projects. You build the one your remainder names. The teaching on the earlier pages is a page that asks and waits. Your project is the system named on Tasks.

0 The shelf      shelf.html
                 board: pen, copy, tea, prices empty until Load
                 till: Loaded, Dearest, Sum, all 0 until Load
                 Load asks for the three prices in one wait
                 Loaded 3, Dearest 80, Sum 170
                 a stopped server blanks the prices and the till

1 The lookup     lookup.html
                 ask, card, slip, figures
                 Find pen: card pen 50, one slip row, Times 1

                 ruler clears the card and leaves the slip

                 ticket, sheet, takings, Drop last
